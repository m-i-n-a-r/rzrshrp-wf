Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I install and use it?

    The instructions are quite straightforward, but if it's the first time you install a custom watch face, you could miss a couple steps:

    • Install the companion app on your smartphone
    • Connect your watch
    • Tap the button in the companion app
    • Check the watch (make sure you're using the same Google account used on the smartphone to purchase the watch face, or you'll have to pay twice!)
    • install the app in the watch
    • Long press on your current watch face
    • Swipe until you see a "+" button
    • Look for the new watch face, tap on it
    • Done, you should see the new watch face
    Another possible approach is to search for the watch face and buy it directly on the watch. It's the same, just a bit cumbersome.

  • Support the [insert non wearOS model] smartwatch!

    All of my watch faces are available on WearOS 3+ devices. This means that they won't run on Tizen and other OSes, they won't run on weird chinese smartwatches from Amazon or Aliexpress, they won't run on your smartphone or on a smartband and so on. A complete list of compatible models would be too long, but to summarize:

    • Any Samsung Watch from the Watch 4
    • Any Mobvoi Ticwatch
    • Latest Fossil smartwatches
    • Oneplus watch
    • Some Oppo and Xiaomi models
    If you can't find your watch in this list, just Google "[your smartwatch name] WearOS" and voilà, you should find if it runs WearOS or not. Another simple test is: does the smartwatch have the Play Store app? Nice, you have WearOS. And you can find my watch faces directly from the watch, if you prefer!

  • Support the [insert non wearOS model] smartwatch!

    Short answer: no. Long answer: my watch faces are programmed, crafted and designed exclusively for WearOS. Porting them to another OS is mostly impossible. BUT, if you want to do so, I can share my assets with you and we could cooperate.

  • I can't install the watch face on my old WearOS smartwatch!

    As I wrote above, most of the watch faces are designed for WearOS 3 and above, so if you still have an old WearOS 2 smartwatch, it sucks but I can't support it, you need a newer model to enjoy my watch faces :(

  • I can't customize the watch face through my phone, it loads forever or crashes the app!

    The inability to customize pretty complex watch faces on the phone is a known bug on Samsung's side, they know that but still they didn't fix it. A workaround until then is to customize the watch face through the watch itself, just long press on it and tap on the customize button! On my side, I have 2 options: remove a lot of customizable elements, or deal with this problem.

  • I installed the companion app on my smartphone, but I have to pay again to install it on the watch

    My guess is that you bought the watch face using a Google account which is not the one you entered in the smartwatch. This may happen if you have more than one account, and the solution is to switch to the correct account. Another way is to buy the watchface on the watch and refund it on the phone connected with the other account.

  • The HR/step goal/anything else isn't displayed correctly

    You can easily fix this by selecting another watch face and setting mine again, or checking the watch face permissions. If the issue persists, send me an email.

  • I want to request a feature!

    Go ahead but please keep in mind that I have a lot of watch faces to maintain and very limited free time, so please be patient. Also, please note that there are thing that simply aren't possible.

  • How do I choose the measuring units?

    The measuging units are chosen automatically and are not manually selectable (at the moment, this may change in the future). The date format and the temperature unit depend on the location, the 12/24 hours format and the distance unit from the language. This may change across watch faces.

Watch Face specific questions

Many frequently asked questions are about a specific watch face, so in this section I'll add detailed information on the watch faces, if needed. If you don't see a watch face here, probably it's pretty self explanatory!

  • Planet in Stars
    Q - What is the circular bar and what does STP and ACC mean?
    A - STP is the step count, if you have a step goal of, say 10k steps, the bar is full when you have 0 steps and empty when you have 10k or more steps. ACC means acceleration, you will see a bar appear when the watch is shaking or accelerating (for example, when you swing your arm).

    Q - Is the compass actually a compass? Can I disable it?
    A - No. The compass is a level on the x axis, and I had to do this because there's no way to access the compass data itself for third party watch faces, as far as I know. You can disable it by customizing the Watch Face and removing the compass ring.

    Q - Can I use the bar to display other data, like the battery level for example?
    A - No, and I'm not sure if this will ever be possible, for technical limitations. Sorry.

  • Programmer
    Q - Can I increase the font size or choose the data I want to display?
    A - No, the layout is fixed. There's an option to make the time bigger and readable though!
